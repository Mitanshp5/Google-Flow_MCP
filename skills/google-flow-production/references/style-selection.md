# Style Selection

Choose by the requested result, not a model name. An explicit non-photoreal direction takes precedence over a product category. Otherwise match the subject, and use social-hook for a short creator-led video without another clear direction. Read only one primary card; at most one secondary card may guide a named beat. Keep the approved story and references authoritative.
