# Real Estate

## Hook
Reveal the genuine defining feature: a room's light, a usable space, a facade, or an authorized view. Orient the viewer before moving through the property.

## Camera
Use a deliberate doorway reveal, a short lateral move, or a restrained room-wide composition. Keep architectural verticals stable and preserve spatial relationships. Separate disconnected rooms with honest cuts.

## Lighting
Match the source property's time of day and window direction. Keep finishes, wall colors, materials, and exterior views faithful to authorized references. Avoid effects that imply nonexistent fixtures or amenities.

## Beat
Establish the property, show the useful spaces in a clear sequence, and finish on the approved identifying feature or invitation. Distinguish a factual listing from a conceptual architectural visualization.

## Avoid
Do not invent floor plans, room dimensions, renovations, neighborhood features, views, or amenities. A generated walkthrough does not prove real spatial connectivity. Label staging or visualization appropriately and review geometry, windows, doors, furniture, and all listing claims.
