# Social Hook

## Hook
Lead with a specific question, recognizable problem, unusual but relevant action, or a direct-address opening. Make the subject and reason to keep watching visible immediately.

## Camera
Use natural framing and modest handheld energy when appropriate. A small push or a quick reveal can support the opening; settle the frame when the viewer needs to understand a demonstration or offer.

## Lighting
Keep faces, hands, and the product readable. Choose a believable setting and consistent practical light rather than an unrelated studio treatment unless the brief asks for it.

## Beat
Open a clear question, show the useful action or answer, and end with one approved next step. Keep dialogue short enough for the verified clip duration. Place essential text within the approved delivery framing.

## Avoid
Do not invent a creator's personal experience, testimonials, statistics, or urgency. A curiosity gap must resolve honestly. Do not promise views, retention, conversion, perfect speech, or readable text from prompt choices; review the actual result.
