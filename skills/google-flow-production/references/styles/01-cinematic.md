# Cinematic

## Hook
Open on a meaningful detail, an unresolved action, or a silhouette that invites a reveal. Establish the subject before adding spectacle.

## Camera
Use a deliberate push-in for realization, a lateral track to connect subject and setting, or a held close-up for an emotional turn. Keep the visual axis and screen direction consistent across cuts.

## Lighting
Choose one motivated key source, readable shadow detail, and a restrained palette. Preserve light direction and time of day between related shots. Atmosphere should reveal depth rather than hide the product.

## Beat
Build from an opening question to an action, then hold its consequence. Contrast a moving reveal with a settled final composition. Give the important image enough time to read.

## Avoid
Do not stack orbit, zoom, whip pan, and transformations in one short shot. Do not imply filmic language unlocks unsupported resolution, frame rate, lens controls, or exact audio. Inspect actual face/product continuity before selecting a clip.
