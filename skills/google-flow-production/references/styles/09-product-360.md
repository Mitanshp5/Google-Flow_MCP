# Product 360

## Hook
Start from an identifying hero angle or a close surface detail that reveals the whole object. Establish the actual silhouette before rotating it.

## Camera
Choose either a controlled object turntable or a camera orbit and state which moves. A partial turn can be more useful than an unverified full rotation. Keep scale and focal position stable.

## Lighting
Use broad highlights to describe curved forms, controlled reflections for glossy surfaces, and a clean background. Maintain material color and avoid reflections that look like extra openings or parts.

## Beat
Show the hero view, reveal one meaningful feature, and return to a stable finish. Use verified angle references for shape consistency when available; inspect intermediate frames rather than assuming endpoints constrain the whole turn.

## Avoid
Do not infer unseen product geometry, ports, logos, or mechanisms. A generated rotation is not a reliable technical inspection or 3D reconstruction. If an exact multi-angle showcase is required, use approved real photographs or renders and an authorized edit.
