# 3D and CGI

## Hook
Reveal scale, material, or a simple assembly action: an object emerges from shadow, parts align, or a surface changes how it catches light.

## Camera
Use a short orbit or controlled fly-through with one readable focal object. Keep perspective and object scale consistent. Separate a camera move from the object's own rotation.

## Lighting
Describe surfaces concretely: brushed metal, frosted glass, soft plastic, or matte ceramic. Use broad reflections to explain shape and a clean background to separate the silhouette.

## Beat
Show an initial state, one transformation or assembly event, and a stable hero view. Reserve material close-ups for the detail that communicates the actual product value.

## Avoid
Do not promise a physically accurate simulation, exact mechanical assembly, hidden product geometry, or an editable 3D model. Stylized rendering is a visual request, not CAD evidence. Review material and shape drift across every cut.
