# Music Video

## Hook
Open on a performance gesture, a striking location detail, or a visual question related to the song's mood. The image should remain meaningful without relying on an unverified timing effect.

## Camera
Use movement that supports the energy: a restrained track for an intimate passage, a wider moving composition for a chorus, or a held reaction at a transition. Keep the performer readable.

## Lighting
Choose a coherent palette and contrast pattern that can evolve with the musical arc. Preserve costume, face, and location continuity even when lighting changes.

## Beat
Decide whether each shot is performance, narrative, or abstract texture. Plan accents against the actual authorized audio in the edit. Record source and usable durations so cuts can be aligned deliberately.

## Avoid
Do not promise automatic beat synchronization, audio upload conditioning, exact lip sync, or rights to a song merely because they are requested in a prompt. Verify actual model support and inspect playback. Precision sync requires a separately approved local edit.
