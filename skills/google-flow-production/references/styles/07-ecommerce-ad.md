# Ecommerce Advertisement

## Hook
Show a recognizable need, an in-hand feature, a texture detail, or a deliberate unboxing reveal. The product should be identifiable before the sequence becomes busy.

## Camera
Use a detail close-up, a restrained reveal pan, or a natural use demonstration. Maintain scale and position between the hand, package, and product. Give the hero view a settled moment.

## Lighting
Use soft, directional light that preserves true product color and material. Keep labels and key shapes visible. Match lighting across creator and product references.

## Beat
Connect a relevant problem to a visible, truthful demonstration, then finish with the approved offer and one action. A short video may combine the reveal and demonstration rather than forcing every narrative beat.

## Avoid
Do not invent discounts, scarcity, reviews, certifications, or before/after results. Do not generate a different product when an accurate reference is supplied. Check fingers, packaging, labels, and geometry; exact branding may require an authorized overlay.
