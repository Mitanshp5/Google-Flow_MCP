# Motion Design Advertisement

## Hook
Reveal a clear problem-to-solution change: clutter resolves into hierarchy, a workflow becomes shorter, or a product screen appears in context. Use only real, approved claims.

## Camera
Use a controlled device reveal, an isometric move, or a simple push toward the relevant interface region. Keep the hierarchy readable and avoid competing simultaneous movements.

## Lighting
Choose a brand-consistent background, restrained accent color, and clear foreground separation. Reflections should not obscure the screen or create false product details.

## Beat
Introduce the use case, demonstrate one benefit, and land on a clean final composition. Plan exact screenshots, labels, metrics, logos, and calls to action as local composites when accuracy matters.

## Avoid
Do not present generated interfaces as a faithful working demonstration. Never invent customer counts, performance gains, charts, or product features. Prompted typography is not guaranteed readable; verify every required word and use an approved edit for precision.
