# Comic to Video

## Hook
Choose one panel's unresolved moment: a gaze, an approaching object, or the instant before an action. The first image should be readable without explaining an entire page.

## Camera
Use a gentle push or pan within a panel, then cut to the next approved composition. Preserve the page's intended reading order and character placement. Keep screen direction coherent.

## Lighting
Maintain the supplied line work, shadow style, palette, and texture. Motion should feel like the illustration continuing, not a sudden change to unrelated photorealism.

## Beat
Map each selected panel to a clear narrative action. Animate expression, cloth, atmosphere, or one gesture while protecting important silhouettes. Plan panel transitions in the edit when continuous interpolation would confuse geography.

## Avoid
Do not assume the generator can parse a crowded comic page or render speech bubbles accurately. Use only authorized source artwork. Important dialogue lettering should be separately composited and checked rather than treated as guaranteed generation output.
