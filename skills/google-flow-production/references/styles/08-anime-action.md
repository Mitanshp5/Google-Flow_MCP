# Anime Action

## Hook
Use a strong pose, a focused expression, a silhouette reveal, or a short anticipatory movement. Establish the emotional intention before an effect-heavy moment.

## Camera
Choose a clear low angle, a close-up-to-wide reveal, or a single tracking move. Preserve character position and action direction. Fast movement works best when the starting and ending poses remain readable.

## Lighting
Choose consistent cel-like shadow shapes, palette, and background treatment. Use rim light or restrained effects to separate the subject without obscuring anatomy or references.

## Beat
Build anticipation, perform one clear movement, and hold the reaction or aftermath. Use motion accents selectively; sustained effects should not replace a coherent action.

## Avoid
Do not promise frame-accurate impact frames, identity locking, or specific animation timing from a prompt. Use authorized original references rather than assuming rights to existing characters. Inspect hands, costume details, props, and expressions between cuts.
