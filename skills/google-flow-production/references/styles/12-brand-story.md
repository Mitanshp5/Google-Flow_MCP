# Brand Story

## Hook
Begin with a real origin detail, an identifiable process, a human decision, or a concrete problem the organization addresses. Prefer evidence over abstract claims of purpose.

## Camera
Use an establishing view to locate the story, a medium view for human action, and a detail shot for process or craft. Keep transitions motivated by the narrative rather than arbitrary camera effects.

## Lighting
Choose a consistent emotional register: warm and personal, restrained and technical, or bright and optimistic. Preserve the same people, spaces, palette, and product appearance across related shots.

## Beat
Connect the initial problem to a real decision and its supported consequence. End with the approved message or invitation. Distinguish documentary source material from illustrative synthetic scenes.

## Avoid
Do not fabricate founders, employees, customers, historical events, certifications, or impact measurements. Do not present an AI reenactment as authentic documentary footage. Obtain approval for likenesses, logos, claims, and the degree of fictional illustration.
