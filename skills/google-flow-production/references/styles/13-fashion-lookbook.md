# Fashion Lookbook

## Hook
Reveal a silhouette, a fabric detail, an accessory, or a deliberate entrance. Let the garment or styling choice read before introducing a second look.

## Camera
Use a natural walk, a restrained partial turn, or a detail-to-full-look progression. Preserve body proportions and keep the garment's important features in frame. Match screen direction across cuts.

## Lighting
Use soft directional light that preserves fabric texture and true color. Keep skin, material, and background separation believable. Maintain wardrobe and accessory continuity within a look.

## Beat
Introduce the look, show how the fabric or accessory moves, then hold an identifying pose. Plan outfit changes as separate reviewed shots when a continuous transformation would obscure product accuracy.

## Avoid
Do not invent fit guarantees, sizes, materials, or product availability. Check garment seams, patterns, logos, jewelry, hands, and body shape. Generated cloth movement is not evidence of real product behavior. Exact catalog accuracy may require real footage or approved still-image editing.
